About

Hi, I'm Amy. I'm a product designer at Buildertrend, where I work on the parts of the platform tied to how builders manage their money, like invoices and job financials.

I tend to care a lot about the details that are easy to overlook. Is this number actually clear? Does someone understand what's going to happen before they click a button? Do they feel confident sending this to their client? To me, those little moments are often the difference between software that just gets the job done and something people actually enjoy using.

A lot of what I know comes from talking directly with the people using what we build. I spend a lot of time with GCs, remodelers, custom home builders, and office managers. They're usually pretty quick to tell me when something doesn't make sense, and those conversations shape a lot of the decisions I make.

Outside of work, I'm usually on a long walk, at the gym, or trying to find an excuse to go fishing.
